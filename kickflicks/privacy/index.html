Kick Flicks Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Version 1.0

Kick Flicks keeps your information on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not collect information about you. There is nothing you need to do.

1. Who we are

Kick Flicks is made by The Digital Citadel Corporation ("we", "us"), 18 King Street East, Suite 1400, Toronto, Ontario, Canada. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, for people in Quebec, Law 25.

2. Information on your phone

Settings, team and score information, and recordings stay on your phone. We do not receive them. You can delete them at any time by deleting them in the app or deleting the app.

3. Information that leaves your phone

Live score sharing is not active yet. No score data leaves your phone. Video you choose to stream goes directly to the streaming server you pick.

4. What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Security

If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people as the law requires.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
