Sleep Train Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what information Sleep Train handles, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. Sleep Train has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your nights stay on your device, and we do not receive them.

1. Who we are

Sleep Train is made by The Digital Citadel Corporation ("we", "us"). This policy covers Sleep Train on iPhone, iPad and Android.

The Digital Citadel Corporation
18 King Street East, Suite 1400
Toronto, Ontario, Canada

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). For people in Quebec, we also follow the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as updated by Law 25. Our Privacy Officer is responsible for this policy.

2. Information we handle

  • Settings on your device. Your choices, such as sound, music, vibration and display, and whether you have seen the opening notices and story. This stays on your device, and we do not receive it.
  • Your nights. A short history of your recent nights, so the app can show your progress. This stays on your device, and we do not receive it.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your message and anything you include in it.
  • Technical information. When you visit our web pages, our hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

The app does not send anything to us or to anyone else. You can’t type anything into it, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. It does not use device or advertising identifiers.

Permissions

Sleep Train uses vibration for gentle cues, and keeps the screen on while a night is running. You can turn vibration off in the app.

Sleep Train does not use your camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or health data.

3. How we use information

Information saved by the app is used only to provide the app's features on your device. We use messages you send us only to respond to you, and we use information where needed to comply with the law.

We do not sell information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. If we want to use information for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

4. How information is shared

  • Service providers. Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores and handle your download under their own privacy policies. GitHub hosts our web pages. None of them receive information from inside the app. If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google may share crash and usage information with us that does not identify you.
  • No ads or analytics. Sleep Train has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social sign-in.
  • Legal reasons. We may share information where the law requires it.
  • Business transfers. If our business or the app is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner, subject to protections at least as strong as this policy.

Some service providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it is subject to local laws and may be accessed by authorities there.

Provider privacy policies:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

5. Not medical advice

Sleep Train is a timer and a story. It isn't medical advice. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and it does not promise any result about sleep. If you have a health concern, please talk to a qualified professional.

6. Retention and security

Settings and your nights stay in the app's private storage on your device until you clear them or delete the app. The app keeps only a limited recent history and drops older nights.

On iPhone and iPad, Apple may include the app's data in your own iCloud or computer backup, if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups under your Apple Account, and we cannot see them. On Android, Sleep Train turns off app backup.

We keep email conversations for up to two years after they end.

Information on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode. We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and limited access, to protect the information we hold. No system is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people as the law requires.

7. Your rights and choices

You can ask to access or correct your information, withdraw your consent, or ask us to delete information we hold. Because the app never sends us your settings or nights, the only information we may hold is email you have sent us. You can clear your nights in the app at any time, delete the app to remove everything, and turn off vibration in the app. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Sleep Train is made for adults. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top when we do. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will let you know, and ask for your consent where the law requires.
