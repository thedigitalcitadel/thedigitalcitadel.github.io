Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and climb aboard. There are no ads, either.

Where do my nights go?

Your nights stay on your device. We do not receive, view or store them.

Can I start fresh?

Yes. You can clear your past nights from the progress screen, or delete the app to remove everything.

Is Sleep Train medical advice?

Sleep Train is a timer and a story. It isn’t medical advice.

How do I report a problem?

Email support with your device, app version and what happened.

Contact

Write to support@thedigitalcitadel.com. Include the app name and your device.
