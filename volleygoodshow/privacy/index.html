Volley Good Show Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what information Volley Good Show handles, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. Volley Good Show has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Most information stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Volley Good Show is made by The Digital Citadel Corporation ("we", "us").

The Digital Citadel Corporation
18 King Street East, Suite 1400
Toronto, Ontario, Canada

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). For people in Quebec, we also follow the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector, as updated by Law 25. Our Privacy Officer is responsible for this policy.

2. Information we handle

  • App data on your device. Settings and the information you enter, such as team, player, score and stats information. This stays on your device, and we do not receive it.
  • Recordings. Recordings you make are saved on your device. We do not receive, view or store them.
  • Live scores you choose to share. If you share a live score, the information needed to show it, along with a random identifier that is not linked to your identity, is stored with our hosting provider. Anyone with the link can view it.
  • Streams. If you stream, your video and audio go directly to the third-party service you choose, not to us.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your message and anything you include in it.
  • Technical information. Our service providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver their services and keep them secure.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts in the app.

Permissions and accessories

The app may ask for access to your camera, microphone and Photos to provide recording features. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

The app may work with compatible accessories you pair with your device. Compatibility and features depend on the accessory and your device settings. Input from accessories is processed on your device.

3. How we use information

We use information only to provide the app's features, to show live scores you choose to share and protect them from misuse, to respond to you, and to comply with the law.

We do not sell information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. If we want to use information for a new purpose, we will ask you first.

4. How information is shared

  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase to host shared live scores, GitHub to host our web pages, and Apple to distribute the app. They receive only what they need to provide their services. If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share crash and usage information with us that does not identify you.
  • Services you choose. Streams and anything you share yourself are handled by the service you choose, under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal reasons. We may share information where the law requires it.
  • Business transfers. If our business or the app is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner, subject to protections at least as strong as this policy.

Some service providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it is subject to local laws and may be accessed by authorities there.

Provider privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Your responsibility for what you record

You choose what and whom you record and share. Please follow the laws and rules that apply where you record, including getting consent where required.

6. Retention and security

App data and recordings stay on your device until you delete them or the app. Shared live scores are deleted shortly after the match, and remaining data is removed by routine cleanup. We keep email conversations for up to two years after they end.

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and limited access, to protect information. No system is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people as the law requires.

7. Your rights and choices

You can ask to access or correct your information, withdraw your consent, or ask us to delete information we hold. Some features may not work if you withdraw consent. You can delete app data in the app or by deleting the app, stop sharing a live score at any time, and manage permissions in your device settings. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

Volley Good Show is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top when we do. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will let you know, and ask for your consent where the law requires.
