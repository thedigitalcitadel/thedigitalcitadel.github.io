Frequently asked questions

How do people follow a game?

The scorer shares a link or shows a QR code. It opens the live score in a phone browser. No app is needed to watch.

Does the store version include stats?

Yes. Player stats and rosters are included.

Where do my recordings go?

Recordings you make are saved on your device. We do not receive, view or store them.

The live score says “Waiting for the game to start”.

The scorer hasn’t started sharing yet, or is using a new link. Ask them for the current link or QR code.

How do I report a problem?

Email support with your device, app version and what happened.

Contact

Write to support@thedigitalcitadel.com. Include the app name and your device.
